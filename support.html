BEFORE YOU SEND

A few details help us investigate.

  1. Name the gameUse the exact title shown in the app store.
  2. Describe the issueTell us what you expected, what happened, and whether it repeats.
  3. Add device detailsInclude the device model, operating system and app version if known.
  4. Protect your informationRemove personal details from screenshots. Do not send passwords or payment card numbers.
